What I'd build from this

  • Nav: two groups, “Price a car” (Import, Market) and “Behind it” (Data, Method, Rates). Rates and the changelog merge into one page; Report a price becomes a button on the car pages.
  • Home: two doors and a strip of real numbers, instead of dropping straight into a form.
  • Market (new): a live scatter of real ads per model with the model line and 80% band. This is where the data science becomes something you touch.
  • Method (new): the data, cleaning funnel, model comparison, interval coverage, learning curve, feature importance and limits, built from the real report files. Terms are explained in plain English next to each chart.
  • Data (new): where each source came from, how collection is kept polite, what is stored (no descriptions, no contact details), and the permissions.
