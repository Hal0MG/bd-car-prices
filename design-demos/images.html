Three ways to show the car

Same four cars, same card, three image sources. Prices are live from the site. Look at how each one handles a JDM-only car (the Axio) next to a famous one (the 911).

A. Real photos from Wikimedia Commons

GoodReal photos of real cars. Free, legal to reuse with a credit line. Pulled automatically from each model's Wikipedia article.

Watch outOften the wrong generation or variant: the Axio search returns a newer Corolla. Backgrounds, angles and quality vary wildly. Each photo needs its own author and licence credit.

EffortMedium. Automatic lookup, then a hand-picked override list for every car that comes back wrong.

B. Studio renders from a car-image API

GoodEvery car at the same angle on a clean background, so the catalogue looks uniform and polished. Can switch colour and angle by URL.

Watch outThis demo uses Imagin.studio's public test key; a live site needs a paid commercial licence. Coverage is built around US/EU catalogues, so Japan-only models (Axio, Premio, Noah) may get a generic stand-in.

EffortLow to build, but a recurring cost and a dependency on someone else's service.

C. Ink drawings by body style, drawn for this site

GoodMatches the ledger look exactly, like a technical drawing on an assessment form. No licences, no outside service, never shows the wrong car. Unique to the site.

Watch outShows the type of car, not the actual model: every SUV gets the same outline. Less eye-catching than a real photo.

EffortLow. About eight drawings cover the whole catalogue.

A + C. A possible mix

Ink drawings as the default everywhere (lists, cards), and a hand-checked photo only on each car's own page, where it's worth verifying it's the right car.
